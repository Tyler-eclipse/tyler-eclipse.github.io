I'm a computer engineering student at Georgia Tech, in the Cybersecurity & Information Internetworks threads. My internship work has ranged from network management software at Ciena, to FPGA-based control systems at Luon Energy, to data tooling at 4Earth — I like projects that move between low-level hardware and the software layered on top of it.
